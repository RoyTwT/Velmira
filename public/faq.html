✨ Products

All products we carry are dermatologist-tested and meet strict safety and efficacy standards. We partner with brands that provide clinical evidence for their formulas, and many are recommended by skincare professionals.
We prioritise cruelty-free brands. Each product page clearly indicates whether it is cruelty-free and/or vegan. Look for the 🐰 and 🌿 badges on product listings. If you're unsure, feel free to contact us.
Take our free Skin Quiz — it takes under 2 minutes and gives you a personalised product routine based on your skin type, concerns, and goals. You can also browse by concern on our Shop page.
Most skincare products have a shelf life of 12–24 months unopened. Once opened, look for the Period After Opening (PAO) symbol on the packaging — it's a small jar icon with a number and "M" (months). We only ship products with at least 6 months remaining before expiry.
Many of our products are formulated for sensitive skin — including our Cetaphil and CeraVe ranges. Each product page lists key ingredients and highlights if it's fragrance-free, hypoallergenic, or suitable for sensitive skin. We recommend doing a patch test before full application.

🌿 Skincare Routine

The general rule is to go from thinnest to thickest consistency. A standard routine looks like:
  • Morning: Cleanser → Toner → Serum → Moisturiser → Sunscreen
  • Evening: Cleanser → Toner → Treatment/Serum → Eye Cream → Moisturiser
Check out our Ingredient Guide for details on layering actives safely.
Skincare takes time. You may notice hydration improvements within a few days, but for concerns like acne, dark spots, or fine lines, it typically takes 4–12 weeks of consistent use to see meaningful results. Patience and consistency are key.
Some actives work well together; others clash. As a general guide:
  • ✅ Vitamin C + SPF — great morning combo
  • ✅ Niacinamide + most actives — very tolerant
  • ⚠️ Retinol + AHA/BHA — can cause irritation; use on alternate nights
  • ⚠️ Vitamin C + Niacinamide — fine for most, but layer separately if sensitive
Visit our Ingredient Guide for a full compatibility chart.
Absolutely — yes. UV damage occurs even on cloudy days and through windows. Daily SPF 30+ is the single most important step in any skincare routine for preventing premature ageing, dark spots, and skin cancer.

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The time it takes to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider — typically another 3–5 business days.
