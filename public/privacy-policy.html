Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we protect it.

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Velmira ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Order information: Products purchased, quantities, and transaction totals.
  • Technical data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, and time on site (via analytics).
  • Preferences: Dark mode preference and skin quiz results stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders.
  • To communicate order status, shipping updates, and receipts.
  • To personalise product recommendations and skincare advice.
  • To improve our website and product offerings.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Storage

Order and cart data are currently stored in your browser's localStorage. This means your data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you complete a purchase. When a backend system is connected, order data will be stored securely on encrypted servers.

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies to maintain session state and track site analytics. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking across other websites. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services including payment processors (for future card payments), shipping partners, and analytics providers. These parties are bound by their own privacy policies and are not permitted to use your data for unrelated purposes.

6. Your Rights

  • Access a copy of your personal data held by us.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

7. Data Retention

We retain order records for up to 7 years as required by financial and tax regulations. Other personal data is deleted within 90 days of account closure or upon request.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us data, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated date. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related queries or to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@velmira.com.
